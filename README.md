EcoHealth Air Pollution Analytics Platform
Project Overview
EcoHealth is a data analytics platform designed to monitor air pollution levels and evaluate their potential impact on public health. The platform integrates environmental datasets, air quality indicators, and analytical dashboards to provide data-driven insights for policymakers, researchers, and health organizations.

Problem Statement
Air pollution is one of the leading environmental risk factors affecting human health worldwide. Understanding pollution trends and identifying high-risk areas are essential for supporting effective environmental and public health decisions.

This project aims to analyze air quality data, uncover pollution patterns, assess environmental risks, and provide actionable insights through interactive visualizations.

Objectives
Monitor air pollution indicators across multiple regions.
Analyze pollution trends over time.
Identify pollution hotspots and high-risk locations.
Support environmental and public health decision-making.
Provide interactive dashboards for data exploration.
